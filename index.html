THE QUESTION

How small can something get before it stops looking like itself?

A photograph holds millions of values. A twenty three brick dog holds twenty three decisions. Everything else gets thrown away, and what survives the throwing away is the part worth arguing about.

Drag any of them to turn all four. Which one stops being your dog?
